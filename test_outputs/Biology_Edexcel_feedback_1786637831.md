### ❓ Question 1 (6 Marks)

#### Question Prompt
```text
Explain how antibodies help protect the body against infection.
```

#### Student Answer
> Neutralisation (Blocking Toxins and Pathogens): Antibodies can bind directly to the surface proteins of viruses or to toxins produced by bacteria. By doing this, they physically block the pathogen from attaching to or entering your body's healthy cells, rendering them harmless.
