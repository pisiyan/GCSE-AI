# GCSE ReligiousStudies (AQA) - Christianity Exam

**Total Marks:** 24

## Topic & Specification Breakdown

### Topic: Nature of God and the Trinity (Specification Code: 3.1.2.1 Beliefs and teachings)
- **Subtopic:** The nature of God as omnipotent, loving and just
- **Subtopic:** The problem of evil and suffering
- **Subtopic:** The oneness of God and the Trinity: Father, Son and Holy Spirit
- **Subtopic:** Different Christian beliefs about creation, including the role of Word and Spirit

### Topic: Incarnation, crucifixion, resurrection and ascension (Specification Code: 3.1.2.1 Beliefs and teachings – Jesus Christ and salvation)
- **Subtopic:** The incarnation and Jesus as the Son of God
- **Subtopic:** The crucifixion, resurrection and ascension
- **Subtopic:** The significance of Jesus' life, death, resurrection and ascension for salvation
- **Subtopic:** The significance of Jesus' life, death, resurrection and ascension for grace
- **Subtopic:** Sin, including original sin
- **Subtopic:** The means of salvation, including law, grace and Spirit
- **Subtopic:** The role of Christ in salvation, including the idea of atonement

### Topic: Prayer and worship (Specification Code: 3.1.2.2 Practices)
- **Subtopic:** Different forms of worship and their significance: liturgical, non-liturgical and informal, including the use of the Bible; private worship
- **Subtopic:** Prayer and its significance, including the Lord’s Prayer, set prayers and informal prayer
- **Subtopic:** The role and meaning of the sacraments: the meaning of sacrament; baptism and its significance for Christians; infant and believers' baptism; different beliefs about infant baptism; Holy Communion/Eucharist and its significance for Christians, including different ways in which it is celebrated and different interpretations of its meaning
- **Subtopic:** The role and importance of pilgrimage and celebrations: two contrasting examples of Christian pilgrimage (Lourdes and Iona); Christmas and Easter, including their importance for Christians in Great Britain today
- **Subtopic:** The role of the Church in the local and worldwide community: food banks and street pastors; mission, evangelism and Church growth; the worldwide Church including reconciliation, responses to persecution, and the work of CAFOD, Christian Aid or Tearfund

### Topic: Sacraments: baptism and Holy Communion (Specification Code: 3.1.2.2 Practices)
- **Subtopic:** Different forms of worship and their significance: liturgical, non-liturgical and informal, including the use of the Bible
- **Subtopic:** Private worship
- **Subtopic:** Prayer and its significance, including the Lord’s Prayer, set prayers and informal prayer
- **Subtopic:** The role and meaning of the sacraments
- **Subtopic:** The meaning of sacrament
- **Subtopic:** The sacrament of baptism and its significance for Christians
- **Subtopic:** Infant and believers' baptism
- **Subtopic:** Different beliefs about infant baptism
- **Subtopic:** The sacrament of Holy Communion/Eucharist and its significance for Christians
- **Subtopic:** Different ways in which Holy Communion/Eucharist is celebrated
- **Subtopic:** Different interpretations of the meaning of Holy Communion/Eucharist

### Topic: Pilgrimage and celebrations (Specification Code: 3.1.2.2 Practices)
- **Subtopic:** Different forms of worship and their significance: liturgical, non-liturgical and informal, including the use of the Bible; private worship
- **Subtopic:** Prayer and its significance, including the Lord’s Prayer, set prayers and informal prayer
- **Subtopic:** The role and meaning of the sacraments: baptism and its significance for Christians; infant and believers' baptism; different beliefs about infant baptism
- **Subtopic:** The sacrament of Holy Communion/Eucharist and its significance for Christians, including different ways in which it is celebrated and different interpretations of its meaning
- **Subtopic:** The role and importance of pilgrimage and celebrations including two contrasting examples of Christian pilgrimage: Lourdes and Iona
- **Subtopic:** The celebrations of Christmas and Easter, including their importance for Christians in Great Britain today

### Topic: The role of the Church in the local and worldwide community (Specification Code: 3.1.2.2 Practices – The role of the church in the local and worldwide community)
- **Subtopic:** The role of the Church in the local community
- **Subtopic:** Food banks
- **Subtopic:** Street pastors
- **Subtopic:** Mission, evangelism and Church growth
- **Subtopic:** The importance of the worldwide Church
- **Subtopic:** Working for reconciliation
- **Subtopic:** How Christian churches respond to persecution
- **Subtopic:** The work of Catholic Agency For Overseas Development (CAFOD), Christian Aid or Tearfund
