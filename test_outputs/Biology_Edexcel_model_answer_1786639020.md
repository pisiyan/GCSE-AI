# Model Answer (Full Marks)
**Question:** Explain how osmosis causes a potato chip to gain or lose mass when placed in a concentrated sugar solution or pure water.

When a potato chip is placed in a concentrated sugar solution, water moves out of the potato cells by osmosis through their partially permeable membranes, from a more dilute solution inside the cells to a more concentrated solution outside, so the chip loses mass. When it is placed in pure water, water moves into the cells by osmosis, from the dilute outside solution to the more concentrated cell sap, so the chip gains mass.
