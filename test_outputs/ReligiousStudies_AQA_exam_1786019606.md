# GCSE ReligiousStudies (AQA) - Islam Exam

**Total Marks:** 48

## Topic & Specification Breakdown

### Topic: beliefs and teachings (Specification Code: 3.1.5.1)
- **Subtopic:** The six articles of faith in Sunni Islam and five roots of Usul ad-Din in Shi’a Islam, including key similarities and differences
- **Subtopic:** Tawhid (the Oneness of God)
- **Subtopic:** Qur’an Surah 112
- **Subtopic:** The nature of God: omnipotence, beneficence, mercy, fairness and justice/Adalat in Shi’a Islam
- **Subtopic:** Different ideas about God’s relationship with the world: immanence and transcendence
- **Subtopic:** Angels, their nature and role, including Jibril and Mika’il
- **Subtopic:** Predestination and human freedom and its relationship to the Day of Judgement
- **Subtopic:** Akhirah (life after death), human responsibility and accountability, resurrection, heaven and hell
- **Subtopic:** Risalah (Prophethood) including the role and importance of Adam, Ibrahim and Muhammad
- **Subtopic:** The holy books
- **Subtopic:** Qur’an: revelation and authority
- **Subtopic:** The Torah, the Psalms, the Gospel, the Scrolls of Abraham and their authority
- **Subtopic:** The imamate in Shi'a Islam: its role and significance

### Topic: practices (Specification Code: 3.1.5.2)
- **Subtopic:** Five Pillars of Sunni Islam and the Ten Obligatory Acts of Shi’a Islam
- **Subtopic:** The Five Pillars and jihad in both Sunni and Shi’a Islam and the additional duties of Shi’a Islam
- **Subtopic:** Shahadah: declaration of faith and its place in Muslim practice
- **Subtopic:** Salah and its significance
- **Subtopic:** How and why Muslims pray including times, directions, ablution (wudu), movements (rak’ahs) and recitations
- **Subtopic:** Salah in the home and mosque and elsewhere
- **Subtopic:** Friday prayer: Jummah
- **Subtopic:** Key differences in the practice of salah in Sunni and Shi’a Islam
- **Subtopic:** Different Muslim views about the importance of prayer
- **Subtopic:** Sawm: the role and significance of fasting during the month of Ramadan
- **Subtopic:** Origins, duties, benefits of fasting, the exceptions and their reasons, and the Night of Power
- **Subtopic:** Qur’an 96:1-5
- **Subtopic:** Zakah: the role and significance of giving alms
- **Subtopic:** Origins, how and why it is given, benefits of receipt, Khums in Shi’a Islam
- **Subtopic:** Hajj: the role and significance of the pilgrimage to Makkah
- **Subtopic:** Origins, how hajj is performed, the actions pilgrims perform at sites including the Ka’aba at Makkah, Mina, Arafat, Muzdalifah and their significance
- **Subtopic:** Jihad: different understandings of jihad
- **Subtopic:** The meaning and significance of greater and lesser jihad
- **Subtopic:** Origins, influence and conditions for the declaration of lesser jihad
- **Subtopic:** Festivals and commemorations and their importance for Muslims in Great Britain today
- **Subtopic:** Id-ul-Adha
- **Subtopic:** Id-ul-Fitr
- **Subtopic:** Ashura
