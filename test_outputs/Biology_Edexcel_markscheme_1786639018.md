**Question (4 marks): TOTAL FOR PAPER = 100 MARKS**

- Award 1 mark for stating that diffusion is the net movement of particles from a higher concentration to a lower concentration.
- Award 1 mark for stating that osmosis is the net movement of water molecules through a partially permeable membrane from a dilute solution to a more concentrated solution.
- Award 1 mark for stating that active transport moves substances from a lower concentration to a higher concentration / against the concentration gradient.
- Award 1 mark for stating that active transport requires energy from respiration.

Mark the answer positively. Credit a correct comparison of the three processes if the relevant points above are made. Do not award marks for vague statements about “movement” without concentration direction or for osmosis if water and/or partially permeable membrane are not mentioned.
