**GCSE Biology (Edexcel) Command Word: _Explain_**

**1. Official Definition & Examiner Expectation**
• **Explain** means: **give reasons why or how something happens**.  
• You must do more than **state facts** or **describe** what is seen.  
• Examiners want a **cause-and-effect chain**:  
  • **Because...**  
  • **Therefore...**  
  • **This means...**  
• In Biology, this often means linking a **biological process** to its **effect**.  
• Full marks usually require **clear scientific reasoning** using correct key terms.

**2. How to Structure Full-Mark Answers**
• Start by **answering the question directly**.  
• Give the **first reason/step**.  
• Add the **next step in the chain**.  
• Finish with the **final effect/outcome**.  
• Use linking phrases such as:  
  • **because**  
  • **this leads to**  
  • **as a result**  
  • **therefore**  
  • **so that**  
• A good answer often follows this pattern:  
  • **Point** → **Reason** → **Scientific explanation** → **Outcome**  
• Keep your answer **logical, precise, and in order**.

**3. Common Student Mistakes & Pitfalls**
• **Describing instead of explaining**  
  • e.g. saying what happens, but not why it happens.  
• **Not using scientific terms**  
  • vague words like “stuff” or “things” lose marks.  
• **Missing the link between ideas**  
  • if the chain of reasoning is broken, the explanation is incomplete.  
• **Giving extra irrelevant detail**  
  • only relevant biology earns marks.  
• **Repetition**  
  • repeating the same idea in different words does not gain extra marks.  
• **One-word answers**  
  • “because it does” is not enough.  
• **Incorrect sequencing**  
  • if steps are out of order, the explanation may not make sense.

**4. Example Demonstration**
• **Incorrect:** “The plant grows more because it gets more light.”  
• **Correct:** “The plant grows more because light increases photosynthesis, so more glucose is made for respiration and growth.”
